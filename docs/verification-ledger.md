# Verification ledger

Status key: SUPPORTED means a primary or reliable secondary source agrees. CORRECTED means my earlier entry was wrong. OPEN means not settled. Nothing here replaces a lawyer's sign-off. Packs stay pending until a reviewer signs them.

## Applied to packs

| Item | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Tamil Nadu: deposit refund disputes go to the Rent Court, not the Rent Authority | SUPPORTED, applied | Act 42 of 2017 as amended. Only the Rent Court hears landlord and tenant disputes (sections 34 and 40). The Rent Authority's powers cover sections 9, 10, 14, 15 and 20 (the 2022 amendment adds 19, but it takes effect only on notification, so confirm). Deposit rule is section 11 |
| Tamil Nadu: deposit cap | CORRECTED | Unless the agreement says otherwise, a deposit above three times the monthly rent is unlawful. It is not simply a default of three months |
| Tamil Nadu: refund timing | CORRECTED | Section 11(2) as amended in 2018: refund when the landlord takes back the vacant premises, not within one month of vacating |
| Tamil Nadu: appeals | ADDED | 30 days from a Rent Authority order to the Rent Court (section 33(2)); 30 days from a final Rent Court order to the Rent Tribunal (section 38(1)); Tribunal decision final (section 38(5)) |
| Keralam: Munsiff's Court limit | SUPPORTED, applied | Kerala Civil Courts Act, 1957, section 13(2), as amended: Munsiff up to 10 lakh rupees. A 2013 amendment raised it from 1 lakh. Above that goes to the Sub Court |
| Keralam: Rent Control Court cannot hear a separate deposit claim | OPEN | Supplied by a reviewer. Consistent with the civil suit route in the pack, but not independently confirmed |

## Corrections to the verification list

| Item | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Carriers Act, 1865 | CORRECTED | Repealed by the Carriage by Road Act, 2007 (Act 41 of 2007). My earlier title "Carriage of Goods by Road Act" was wrong. Section 16: written notice within 180 days of booking, for loss of or damage to a consignment only. Sources differ on the commencement date (20 July 2010 or 1 March 2011), so leave it open |
| Mediation Act, 2023 | SUPPORTED | Section 5(1): pre-litigation mediation is voluntary. The proviso sends commercial disputes of specified value to section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015, which is mandatory unless urgent interim relief is sought |
| Labour codes | SUPPORTED with caveats | All four codes (Wages, Social Security, Industrial Relations, Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions) took effect on 21 November 2025 and the 29 central laws are repealed. Some provisions, such as parts relating to the EPF Act, and several state rules were still pending. Check the specific provision and state before relying on it |
| Tamil Nadu Apartment Ownership Act | CORRECTED | The 1994 Act is repealed. The Tamil Nadu Apartment Ownership Act, 2022 (Act 44 of 2022) took effect on 6 March 2024, with rules notified on 24 September 2024 |
| Gujarat rent law | PARTLY CORRECTED | I found no "Rent Control Act, 1999". India Code lists the Gujarat Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act, 1947. It lapsed on 31 March 2021, and a 2024 Bill revived it from 1 April 2021. I did not confirm that the Bill was enacted |
| Telangana land records | CORRECTED twice | The 1971 Act was replaced by the 2020 Act, which was itself repealed and replaced by the Telangana Bhu Bharati (Record of Rights in Land) Act, 2025, effective 14 April 2025 |
| West Bengal court fees | SUPPORTED | West Bengal Court-fees Act, 1970 (West Bengal Act X of 1970), amended several times, including 2002 |
